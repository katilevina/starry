# Cover Letter Template

**⚠️ SYNC RULE:** This template must be kept in sync with `applications/my_data/template_cover_letter.md`

---

## Purpose

Template for writing effective cover letters that complement your CV and demonstrate genuine interest in the role.

---

## Template Structure

### Subject Line (для email)

**Format:** `[Role Title] application - [Your Name]`

**Example:** `Senior Product Manager application - Jane Doe`

---

### Body

#### 1. Интродакшн

**Format:** Hi [Hiring Manager Name],

I'm writing to express my interest in the [Role Title] position at [Company Name].

**Personalized alternative:** If you found something specific in your research about the company or the hiring manager, mention it here.

---

#### 2. Почему вы подходите (2-3 bullet points)

Based on the job requirements, I believe I'm a strong fit because:

* **[Requirement 1 from JD]:** [Specific achievement with metric] — [Result]
* **[Requirement 2 from JD]:** [Specific achievement with metric] — [Result]
* **[Requirement 3 from JD]:** [Specific achievement with metric] — [Result]

**Examples:**

❌ "3 years of content writing experience"
✅ "Wrote 47 articles for TechCorp, generating 50K+ monthly views"

❌ "Experience managing teams"
✅ "Led a team of 5 content writers, increasing output by 2.3× while maintaining quality"

❌ "Background in B2B marketing"
✅ "Developed B2B content strategy for LinkedIn that drove 40% increase in lead generation"

---

#### 3. Интерес (Motivation)

**I'm excited about this opportunity because:**

[Choose 1-2 specific reasons based on your research]

**Templates:**

- **Product/mission alignment:** "I've been following [Company]'s work in [industry/domain] and really admire [specific product/initiative/mission aspect]."
- **Growth phase:** "The opportunity to join [Company] at this [growth stage/expansion phase] is particularly appealing to me because [reason]."
- **Specific challenge:** "I'm drawn to the challenge of [specific problem mentioned in JD] that your team is solving."
- **Industry/domain:** "My background in [your domain] makes me particularly excited to contribute to [company's domain/mission]."

**⚠️ Important:** Avoid generic statements like "I've always wanted to work at your company." Be specific.

---

#### 4. Индивидуальный заход (опционально)

**Personal connection:** [Optional: brief personal fact that relates you to the company/role]

**Examples:**

- "As someone who [personal fact related to company's mission/product], I've personally experienced the value of [what company does]."
- "I've been using [product] for [time period] and was excited to see the recent [feature/launch]."
- "Your recent blog post about [topic] really resonated with my experience at [previous company]."

**⚠️ Note:** Only include if genuine. Don't force it.

---

#### 5. Call to Action

**I'd welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background aligns with [Company]'s goals.**

**Best regards,**
[Your Name]
[Your Email]
[Your Phone]
[LinkedIn Profile URL]

**Alternative CTA:**

- "I'd love to chat about how I can contribute to [specific team/project mentioned in JD]."
- "I'm available for a call this week to discuss the role in more detail."

---

## Quality Checklist

Before sending:

- [ ] Read the JD carefully and addressed top 3 requirements
- [ ] Each bullet point includes a specific metric/achievement
- [ ] Researched the company and mentioned something specific
- [ ] Did NOT duplicate CV content (complemented it instead)
- [ ] Did NOT focus on own problems/challenges
- [ ] Did NOT use generic "always wanted to work here" without specifics
- [ ] Proofread for grammar (use Grammarly for English)
- [ ] Explained any nonlinear career path (if applicable)
- [ ] Explained why applying to this industry/domain (if not obvious)
- [ ] Tone is human, not robotic or overly formal
- [ ] No fluff — every sentence adds value
- [ ] Length: 150-250 words (keep it concise)

---

## Language Notes

**Russian vs English:**

- **Russian:** Can be slightly more informal ("ты" if company culture permits), but still professional
- **English:** Professional but warm — avoid overly formal language, but don't be too casual

**Tone matching:**

- Read the job description tone — is it formal/corporate or casual/startup?
- Match your CL tone accordingly
- When in doubt, professional-but-friendly is safest

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Generic:** "I believe my qualifications make me a strong candidate" (everybody says this)
❌ **Self-focused:** "I'm looking for a role that will help me grow my skills in X" (employer doesn't care about your growth)
❌ **Vague:** "I have experience in content marketing" (what does this mean? Be specific)
❌ **Repetitive:** Repeating the exact bullets from your CV (CL should complement, not duplicate)
❌ **Long:**超过300 слов (nobody reads long cover letters)

✅ **Specific:** "I wrote 47 articles that generated 50K monthly views"
✅ **Company-focused:** "I'm excited to help [Company] achieve [specific goal mentioned in JD]"
✅ **Concrete:** Include numbers, names, results
✅ **Complementary:** Highlight aspects that aren't fully captured in CV format
✅ **Concise:** 150-250 words, use bullets for readability
