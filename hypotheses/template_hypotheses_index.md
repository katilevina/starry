## Validation Rules

Each hypothesis goes through:
1. **Define** — what we're testing, success criteria
2. **Execute** — outreach, applications, conversations
3. **Validate** — results vs criteria, lessons learned
4. **Decide** — continue, pivot, or stop

**Success criteria examples:**
- Response rate > X% from cold outreach
- At least Y companies invite to interview
- Consistent feedback pattern (e.g., "we need someone more X")
- Job offer from at least 1 company
